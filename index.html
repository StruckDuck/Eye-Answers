The Eye Knows All

What it looks like

Ask a friend to think of something. A name, a color, a city. Have them write it down so that The Eye can’t see or hear what they’ve chosen.

You type a question into your phone, right in front of them, something like “Can you guess what color Whitney is thinking of?” Then you poke the eye.

It closes. It opens. Then it says, in a full sentence, exactly what they were thinking.

How it works

The Eye doesn’t read minds. You do all the work, and nobody sees it. Make sure you see what your friend writes down before you start.

Next to the question box is a secret button disguised as a gross zit. Tap it, and everything you type after is quietly saved as the secret answer, while the screen types out an innocent question for you. Tap it again to lock the secret in, finish your real question, and poke the eye. You have 37 characters including spaces to type the answer.

To everyone watching, you only typed a question. The Eye reveals the answer you secretly typed.

Advanced Handling

Sentence answers

If you enter a secret and your question contains one of these category words, The Eye answers in a full sentence that is relevant to that category.

Name, Number, Color, Month, Day,
Animal, City, Food, Year, Card

Examples:

  • Can you guess what name they are thinking of?
  • Can you guess what color Whitney is picturing?
  • Can you guess Rusty’s favorite animal?

Year

This one is also useful for anyone whose phone keyboard separates the number and letter keys.

Simply spell out the last two digits of whatever year you want The Eye to guess within the past 100 years. Spell a number lower than the current year’s last two digits and The Eye guesses that year from the 2000’s, so “thirteen” shows 2013. Anything higher gives the 1900s, so “seventy five” shows 1975.

Card

For Card, type the rank and the suit of the card you want The Eye to guess. For the ace of hearts type “a h”, for the seven of clubs type “seven c”, jack of diamonds type “j d”, for the ten of spades type “ten s”.

Single letters work for the suit (h, d, c, s) and for face cards (a, j, q, k), so “k h” is the King of Hearts. The Eye answers with the rank and the suit’s symbol, like 7 ♣ or Queen ♦.

Numbers

This is useful for anyone whose phone keyboard separates the number and letter keys. Switching to numbers while seemingly typing a question could look suspicious if noticed.

Simply spell out any number between 0-100. If you type “twelve”, The Eye shows 12. If you type “seventy five” or “seventyfive” The Eye will show 75. Don’t worry about misspelling a number, The Eye knows all.

Yes/no questions

If you don’t enter a secret and ask a yes/no question, The Eye answers like a magic 8-ball.

Everything else

If you don’t enter a secret and the question isn’t yes/no, The Eye gives one of its stock phrases.

The zit
  • Tap the zit before you type the secret.
  • Tap the zit again, or double-tap the space bar, to lock the secret in.
Add to your home screen

Add the web app to your home page to access The Eye anytime you want!

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.
