Ask the eye a question,
Poke the eye for your answer.

How this actually works

  1. Before typing, tap the zit (above and to the right of the eye).
  2. Type the secret answer. "Can you guess what" will spell itself out on screen automatically while you're actually typing the real answer underneath.
  3. When you're done typing the answer, tap the zit again to lock it in.
  4. Finish the sentence however you like, normally, then poke the eye. It will reveal whatever you secretly typed as the answer.
