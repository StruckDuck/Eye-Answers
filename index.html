Ask the eye a question,
Poke the eye for your answer.

How this actually works

  1. Tap the zit. It's the bump above and to the right of the eye. Nothing will appear to happen.
  2. Tap the question box and type the secret. The box spells “Can you guess what they are thinking?” no matter what you type. You have 37 characters.
  3. Tap the zit again, or double-tap the space bar. Either one locks the secret in.
  4. Finish the question. Type whatever you like to end the question.
  5. Backspace counts as a keystroke. It deletes a letter from the secret, but the sentence keeps spelling forward.

Dig deeper

Numbers

Type numbers as words. If you type “twelve”, the eye shows 12. This means you never have to switch your keyboard to the number pad while entering the secret, which could look suspicious. It works from 0 to 100 and forgives misspellings. On Android the keyboard has a number row built in, so digits can be typed straight in, and the period key also locks the secret.

Sentence answers

If you enter a secret and your question contains one of these categories, the eye answers in a full sentence.

Name, Number, Color, Month, Day, Animal, City, Food, Year, Card

Examples:

  • Can you guess what name they are thinking of?
  • Can you guess what color Whitney is picturing?
  • Can you guess what city Rusty is from?

For Year, you only need to type two digits as words. Up to this year’s last two digits gives the 2000s, so “thirteen” shows 2013. Anything higher gives the 1900s, so “seventy five” shows 1975. It reaches back 100 years.

For Card, use the singular word “card” in your question, then type the rank and the suit, like “ace h”, “seven c”, “jack d” or “ten s”. Letters work for the suit (h, d, c, s) and for face cards (a, j, q, k), so “k h” is the King of Hearts. The eye answers with the rank and the suit’s symbol, like 7 ♣ or Queen ♦.

Yes/no questions

If you don’t enter a secret and ask a yes/no question, the eye answers like a magic 8-ball.

Everything else

If you don’t enter a secret and the question isn’t yes/no, the eye gives one of its stock phrases.

The zit
  • Tap it before you type the secret.
  • Tap it again, or double-tap the space bar, to lock the secret in.
