I see all. Past, present, and future.
Trinkets and hollow words do not move me.
Name a worthy gift, then ask your question, and I shall answer true.

How this actually works

  1. Before typing your offering, tap the zit (above and to the right of the eye).
  2. Type the answer to the question that will be asked. The offering phrase will spell itself out automatically while you're actually typing the answer.
  3. When you're done entering the answer, tap the zit again, then finish the phrase by naming an actual item to offer.
  4. Enter the question below the eye and poke it. The eye will reveal whatever you secretly typed.
